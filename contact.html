Press & partnerships

For interviews, embargoed product news, or partnership proposals, write to elefnarin@gmail.com with the subject line Press or Partnership. We answer press inquiries within 24 hours on weekdays. A press kit with high-resolution logos, mascot artwork, and approved product screenshots is available on request and will be linked from this page in a future update.

App support

For in-app questions, account help, billing, or data requests, please use the in-app support link in the relevant app (Settings → Help → Contact us). The in-app channel is the fastest way to reach the team that maintains the product. For privacy and data-deletion requests, see our Privacy Policy.

Where we are

Europe, but always-on.

Our team is distributed across the EU, the UK, and Canada. We work in CET business hours, and we have async overlap with North America by design. Remote-first means no offices to visit, but every team member is reachable on a video call within a day. 🌍
